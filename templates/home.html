FEATURES

Everything built from scratch — for your clients

No third-party voice platforms. Every feature is custom-built, fully owned by you.

Inbound Call Handling

Answer every call instantly. Your AI agent greets callers, understands their intent, and takes action — booking, FAQ, or handoff.

Room & Table Booking

Agents check availability and confirm room stays or restaurant tables — logged to your bookings board automatically.

Outbound Campaigns

Upload a CSV, schedule a campaign, and your agent calls every contact automatically with a personalized script.

Upload FAQs, pricing sheets, and policy docs. Your agent searches them in real-time to answer questions accurately.
